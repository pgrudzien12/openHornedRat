## GitHub Discussion: Welcome, early testers

**Title:** Early playtest: help us test Open Horned Rat

Open Horned Rat is an early reimplementation of *Warhammer: Shadow of the Horned Rat* for modern systems. The current source-only prototype can run the opening campaign flow and a playable first battle, but it is far from a complete replacement for the original game.

We are looking for testers with a legal local copy of the original game, especially people willing to explore the campaign screens and play battles long enough to find problems. Please report startup issues, broken flow, input/camera problems, visual or audio glitches, crashes, and battle behaviour that feels wrong. Include your OS, original game edition, start command, and the generated battle log where possible.

Start here: `REPOSITORY_URL`

The first packaged playable release is targeted for the end of October 2026. Until then, the README explains how to run the source build. Please do not upload or share original game assets.

## Reddit post

**Title:** Looking for early testers: Open Horned Rat, a modern reimplementation of Shadow of the Horned Rat

I’m opening **Open Horned Rat**, an early fan-made reimplementation of *Warhammer: Shadow of the Horned Rat* (1995). It currently runs from source and is useful for testing the opening campaign flow and BF001, the first battle: selecting regiments, moving, charging, shooting, basic morale/routing, enemy AI, and battle results all work in prototype form.

It is not a finished game or a drop-in replacement yet. The rules, campaign screens, audio/presentation, saves, and full campaign are still in progress. The goal is a first packaged playable release around the end of October 2026.

If you own the original game (GOG v1.0 is the tested version) and are happy to test an early build, reports from Linux, Windows, and other editions would be hugely useful—especially for campaign flow, crashes, controls, and anything that differs from the original. The repository contains no game assets; you point it at your own legal installation.
